What We Do

Five services, one steady hand.

Every service below follows the same thread: clean technique, no rush, and results that last. Ask in salon for full pricing.

Eyebrow Threading

Our specialty. Sharp, symmetrical shaping using traditional cotton-thread technique — gentler on skin than wax, precise down to a single hair.

Henna Tattoo

Facial Skin Care

Cleansing, exfoliating facials suited to your skin type, made to leave you glowing before an event — or just for yourself.
